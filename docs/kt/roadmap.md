# MVP roadmap

The `Phase` field of each requirement refers to these phases. `later` means after phase 4.

| Phase | Theme | Deliverables | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research ingestion | OpenAlex integration, arXiv integration, PDF storage, metadata extraction | REQ-DATA-001, REQ-DATA-002, REQ-DATA-004, REQ-DATA-005 |
| 2 | Research understanding | Embeddings, vector database, semantic search, opportunity extraction | REQ-PROC-001, REQ-PROC-002, REQ-PROC-003, REQ-PROC-004 |
| 3 | AI opportunity generation | Startup Generator, Market Analyzer, Competitor Scanner | REQ-AGENT-003, REQ-AGENT-004, REQ-AGENT-005 |
| 4 | Business intelligence | Scoring engine, dashboard, opportunity ranking, trend analysis | REQ-SCORE-001, REQ-SCORE-002, REQ-DASH-001, REQ-DASH-002, REQ-DASH-003 |

The roadmap does not yet assign a phase to Semantic Scholar (REQ-DATA-003), the Research Analyst, Problem Discovery, and Relevance agents (REQ-AGENT-001, REQ-AGENT-002, REQ-AGENT-006). Those requirements have `Phase: later` until the team assigns one.
